# Grading Model

The course separates short labs and four major assignments.

| Assignment | Title | Core evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | NFT Trading + Special Contract | ERC721 events and contract interaction |
| 2 | Ethernaut | LevelCompletedLog events and complexity score ≥ 10 |
| 3 | Multi-chain Contract Chain | Ethereum tx + Polkadot extrinsic + TON tx |
| 4 | Technical Article | Published URL and technical quality |

Checks are deterministic, semi-deterministic, or instructor/LLM-assisted.
