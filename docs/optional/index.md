# Optional / Advanced Tasks

This section contains tasks that were removed from the required course path.

They are still useful, but they should not be mandatory for every student.

## Optional Task List

| Task | Best For | Recommended Status |
|---|---|---|
| Lab 7 — DEX arbitrage | DeFi-interested students | Bonus |
| Lab 9 — AssetHub / XCM | Polkadot-interested students | Bonus or demo |
| Lab 11 — Jetton transfer | TON-interested students | Bonus / guided report |
| Lab 12 — TON DeFi / Security | Strong students | Advanced bonus |
| Assignment 1 — NFT Quest | Students who liked Lab 6 | Bonus |
| Assignment 2 — Ethernaut | Security-interested students | Bonus |

## Instructor Recommendation

Do not assign all optional tasks at once.

Good options:

```text
Choose any 1 optional task for bonus points.
```

or:

```text
Choose one track:
- DeFi track: Lab 7
- Security track: Assignment 2
- Polkadot track: Lab 9
- TON track: Lab 11 or Lab 12
```
