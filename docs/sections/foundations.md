# Section 1 — Blockchain Foundations

This section introduces the basic building blocks of blockchain systems.

Students learn:

- what a wallet is;
- how transactions work;
- why blockchains use hashes;
- how Merkle trees summarize transactions;
- how mining and difficulty work.

## Learning Sequence

The first three missions are intentionally cumulative:

1. **Mission 1 — Enter the Blockchain:** create and inspect a basic transaction.
2. **Mission 2 — Hash Detective:** observe SHA-256, find a collision in a deliberately weak hash, and learn why collision resistance matters.
3. **Mission 3 — Mine Your First Block:** reuse SHA-256 to build a Merkle root, construct a block header, and find a nonce with Proof of Work.

Mission 2 supplies the vocabulary and evidence used in Mission 3. Mission 3 then replaces the small educational hash with deterministic SHA-256 rules for the Merkle tree and block header.

## Labs

- Lab 1 / Mission 1 — Enter the Blockchain.
- Lab 2 / Mission 2 — Hash Detective.
- Lab 3 / Mission 3 — Mine Your First Block.
