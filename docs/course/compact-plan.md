# Compact Course Plan

This is the recommended reduced version of the course.

It keeps the strongest learning path and removes overload.

## Required Core

| Order | Item | Main Skill | Estimated Time |
|---:|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Lab 1 — Enter the Blockchain | Wallet, Sepolia transaction | 0.5–1.5 h |
| 2 | Lab 2 — Hash Detective | Hashing and collision intuition | 1–2.5 h |
| 3 | Lab 3 — Mine Your First Block | Merkle tree, simple mining | 2–5 h |
| 4 | Lab 4 — Read the Chain | Ethereum transactions and gas | 1–2.5 h |
| 5 | Lab 5 — Launch Your Token | ERC20, transfers, Disperse | 2–5 h |
| 6 | Lab 6 — Create and Move NFTs | ERC721, approval, transfer | 3–6 h |
| 7 | Lab 8 — Enter Polkadot | Account, extrinsic, explorer | 1.5–3 h |
| 8 | Lab 10 — Enter TON | TON wallet, testnet tx, explorer | 1–2.5 h |
| 9 | Assignment 3 — Group Chain of Contracts | Team Sepolia contract chain | 4–10 h per student |
| 10 | Assignment 4 — Web3 Technical Article | Technical explanation | 3–6 h |

## Why This Version Works

This version keeps one clear path:

```text
Basic blockchain mechanics
  ↓
Ethereum transactions
  ↓
Tokens and NFTs
  ↓
Alternative blockchain ecosystems
  ↓
Group contract interaction
  ↓
Final technical explanation
```

Students still see Bitcoin-style foundations, Ethereum/EVM, ERC20, ERC721, Polkadot, TON, group smart contract interaction, and technical writing.
