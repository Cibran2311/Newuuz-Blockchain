# What Can Be Reduced

This page shows what to cut first if the course becomes too heavy.

---

## Cut Order

| Priority | Item | Recommendation | Reason |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lab 12 — TON DeFi / Security | Move to bonus | High complexity, unstable ecosystem tooling |
| 2 | Lab 9 — AssetHub / XCM | Make instructor demo or bonus | XCM is conceptually hard and tooling-heavy |
| 3 | Assignment 2 — Ethernaut | Reduce complexity score | Time can grow unpredictably |
| 4 | Lab 7 — DEX arbitrage | Use guided/simplified version | Good topic, but math + contracts can overload students |
| 5 | Assignment 1 — NFT Quest | Merge with Lab 6 | Same conceptual area: NFT ownership, approvals, transfers |
| 6 | Lab 11 — Jetton transfer | Make guided report | Useful, but can be shorter |
| 7 | Assignment 4 — Article | Shorten length | Keep it, but reduce size if needed |

---

## What Should Not Be Cut

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| Lab 1 | Students need wallet and testnet basics |
| Lab 2 | Hashing is foundational |
| Lab 3 | Blocks and Merkle trees explain blockchain structure |
| Lab 4 | Students must read transactions and gas |
| Lab 5 | ERC20 gives first practical smart contract skill |
| Lab 6 | NFTs teach ownership, approvals, token IDs |
| Lab 8 | Gives non-Ethereum perspective |
| Lab 10 | Gives TON perspective |
| Assignment 3 | Group chain creates real interaction and teamwork |

---

## Best Short Version

For a compressed course, use:

```text
Lab 1, Lab 2, Lab 3,
Lab 4, Lab 5, Lab 6,
Lab 8, Lab 10,
Assignment 3,
Assignment 4
```

This keeps the course coherent:

```text
Blockchain basics → Ethereum contracts → alternative ecosystems → group contract workflow → final explanation
```

---

## Best Full Version

For a full semester, use:

```text
Lab 1–8,
Lab 10–11,
Assignment 1–4,
Lab 7 simplified,
Lab 9 and Lab 12 as bonus or advanced
```

This gives a strong balance between breadth and workload.
