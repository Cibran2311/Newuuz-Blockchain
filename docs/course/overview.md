# Course Overview

The course is divided into four sections.

## Section 1 — Blockchain Foundations

Students learn wallets, transactions, hash functions, Merkle trees, and Proof-of-Work.

## Section 2 — Ethereum and Smart Contracts

Students work with Ethereum transactions, ERC20, NFTs, AMMs, DEX arbitrage, and Ethernaut.

## Section 3 — Polkadot and Cross-chain Systems

Students learn Polkadot accounts, extrinsics, AssetHub, parachains, and XCM.

## Section 4 — TON and Asynchronous Blockchain Architecture

Students work with TON wallets, jettons, message traces, TON DeFi, and security challenges.

## Page format

Each lab includes:

1. Goal.
2. Why This Lab Matters.
3. What You Will Learn.
4. Required Reading.
5. Required Software.
6. Key Terms.
7. Safety Notes.
8. Step-by-Step Instructions.
9. Expected Result.
10. Submission.
11. Automatic Validation.
12. Common Mistakes.
13. Self-Check Questions.
