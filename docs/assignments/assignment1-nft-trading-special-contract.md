# Assignment 1 — NFT Trading + Special Contract

## Goal
Work with NFT ownership, approvals, marketplace-style transfer logic, and special smart contract interaction.

## Tasks
1. Buy or receive the professor NFT.
2. Transfer or sell the professor NFT back according to class instructions.
3. Mint your own ERC721 NFT.
4. Approve the special contract to transfer your NFT.
5. Send your NFT to the special contract.
6. Submit all transaction hashes in `submission.json`.

## Automatic validation
The checker verifies NFT transfer events, ownership changes, mint event, and transfer to the special contract.
